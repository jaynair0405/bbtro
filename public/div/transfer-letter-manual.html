1 What is this feature?

Two minutes of background — why this exists.

When staff are transferred from one lobby to another, the sending lobby issues a transfer letter to the receiving lobby. Earlier, every lobby had its own format in Word/Excel, and the receiving lobby then had to transfer each staff into their lobby one at a time on the portal.

The Transfer Letter feature replaces all of that:

  • One standard format for the whole division — same header, same table, same look, always.
  • The letter PDF is filed automatically in Documents → Transfer Letters, sorted by lobby and month.
  • One click transfers all the staff on the letter to the receiving lobby — they appear in that lobby's Staff Transfers inbox automatically.
  • The receiving lobby accepts everyone on the letter itself — fill CMS IDs, press one button.
  • Every letter is saved forever and can be reopened, reprinted or downloaded any time.

3 Opening the page & knowing your role

Who sees what.

From the Division portal dashboard, click 📨 Prepare Transfer Letter in the left sidebar (just below Staff Transfers).

  • Lobby office user (SRCC login): the "From" lobby is fixed to your own lobby — you prepare letters going out of your lobby, and accept staff coming into it.
  • Division Admin: can prepare a letter from any lobby to any lobby (the From dropdown is open), and can also un-finalize a letter if a mistake is found (see FAQ).

The page has two halves: the form on the left, and a live preview of the actual letter on the right — whatever you type appears on the letter instantly, exactly as it will print.

At the top you'll always see the letter's journey: Draft → Finalized · PDF filed → Staff Transferred

4 Preparing a letter (sending lobby)

Section by section, top to bottom.

Panel 1 — Offices & Letter

To (receiving lobby) Choose where the staff are going. The addressee block and subject line fill themselves — you can still edit both.
Letter No. A number is suggested automatically (e.g. SR CC/TFR/IGP/2026/03) and increases with every letter. It is fully editable — if your office follows its own numbering, simply type over it.
Letter Date & Transfer Category Date defaults to today. Category (Permanent Transfer / Promotion / Temporary Transfer) applies to all staff on the letter and decides how the receiving side records them.
Ref. Type the office order number and date, e.g. "Office Order No.142/2026/ALP dated 10/06/2026".

Panel 2 — Staff on this letter

Search and add staff Type a name, PF number, HRMS ID or CMS ID (2+ characters) and click the staff in the dropdown. Repeat for everyone — the cursor stays in the search box so you can add 15 staff in under a minute. Only active staff of the sending lobby appear.
Set dates ONCE on the first staff Fill Relieving date and Reporting date (new lobby) on the first staff — they copy to every other staff automatically (and to staff you add later). Then change only the exceptions. Rows you've hand-edited are never overwritten.
Per-staff details Reporting date (existing lobby) fills automatically from records. Footplate KM is optional — type it if your lobby records it, else leave blank. Remarks is optional (e.g. "Availed Rest on 05-07-2026") — it prints as an extra column only if at least one staff has a remark.
Remove someone Click the ✕ on their card. Serial numbers renumber automatically.

Must-fill rule Before finalizing, every staff needs a relieving date and a new-lobby reporting date. Everything else can be blank.

Panel 3 — Letter text

The office header, body paragraph, footnote bullets, closing line, signing designation and C/- (copy to) list all come pre-filled with the division's standard wording. Edit any of them for this letter — your edits affect this letter only; the next letter starts from the standard again.

Saving

Press Save Draft as often as you like. A draft can be reopened from Letters History → 📤 Prepared, edited, or deleted. Nothing has gone anywhere yet — a draft is completely private to the portal.

You can 🖨 Print at any stage to see the paper output — letters with up to about 15 staff print on one page; longer letters continue on page 2 with the table header repeated and the signature at the end.

5 Finalize & File PDF

Making the letter official.

When the letter is complete, press Finalize & File PDF and confirm. Two things happen:

  1. The letter becomes read-only — its contents are now the official record.
  2. A PDF of the letter is generated and filed automatically into Documents → Transfer Letters → your lobby → year → month, visible to every logged-in user of the portal.

Print it, get it signed and stamped, and dispatch the physical copy exactly as you do today. The portal copy and the paper copy are the same letter.

Made a mistake after finalizing? A lobby user cannot unlock a finalized letter. Ask the Division Admin — they can revert it to Draft (this also removes the filed PDF) as long as the staff have not been transferred yet. After transfer, the letter is permanent.

6 Transfer staff now

The one click that replaces all the manual work.

Once finalized, the red button Transfer staff now → becomes active, with an explanation right above it. Pressing it (and confirming — the box lists every staff by name) does this:

  • Every staff on the letter gets a pending transfer request to the receiving lobby — the same kind the portal has always used, just all at once.
  • The receiving lobby's Staff Transfers badge on their dashboard counts up immediately.
  • The letter status becomes Transferred — permanent.

All-or-nothing safety If even one staff already has some other pending transfer request, no requests are created at all and the letter tells you exactly which staff are blocked and why. Resolve those (usually: the old request needs accepting or rejecting) and press the button again.

Important Nothing changes for the staff yet — they still belong to your lobby. The actual change of lobby happens only when the receiving lobby accepts each staff on reporting. Until then they are simply "in transit" (pending).

7 Receiving lobby — your side of the story

How you know a letter has come, and where to find it.

Two signals tell you staff are on their way:

  • The Staff Transfers badge on your dashboard sidebar shows the pending count — as always.
  • On the Transfer Letter page, open Letters History → 📥 Received. Every letter addressed to your lobby is listed with who sent it and a live chip: ⏳ 4 to accept means four staff on that letter haven't been accepted yet; ✓ all accepted means the letter is fully processed.

Click any received letter to open it. You see the full letter exactly as the sending lobby printed it — you can 🖨 Print it or open/save the filed 📄 PDF for your records.

8 Accepting reported staff on the letter

No more dashboard → transfers → accept, one by one.

When you open a received letter, the left side shows the green "Accept reported staff" panel — every staff on the letter with two boxes:

New CMS ID Fill it for each staff who has physically reported at your lobby (letters followed by digits, e.g. KYN1234). Leave it blank for staff who haven't reported yet — that is the whole trick.
Reported on Pre-filled with the reporting date written on the letter. If the staff actually reported on a different day, change it. This date goes into the staff's service record and will appear automatically as their "reporting date of existing lobby" on any future transfer letter.
Press Accept Accept reported staff (4 pending) — confirm, done. Each filled staff is transferred into your lobby with their new CMS ID. Any problem with one staff (say, a CMS ID already in use) is shown under that staff and does not stop the others.
The rest come later Staff you left blank stay ⏳ Pending. When they report next week, open the same letter from the Received tab (the chip reminds you how many are left) and accept them the same way. Repeat until the chip says ✓ all accepted.

Same rules as before The acceptance itself is identical to the old dashboard accept — CMS ID checks, records, history — just done for many staff at once, from the letter. The old dashboard accept also still works if you prefer it for a single staff.

10 Where to find letters & PDFs later

  • Letters History (on the page) — 📤 Prepared shows every letter your lobby made; 📥 Received shows every letter sent to you. Click to reopen and reprint — years later too.
  • Documents — Dashboard → Documents → 📨 Transfer Letters → (sending lobby) → year → month. The auto-filed PDFs live here and every logged-in user can open or download them. Offices may also upload the signed/stamped scan of a letter here for record.
  • Staff record — the transfer itself (from, to, category, dates, reporting date at the new lobby) is written to the staff's transfer history, visible in the biodata form's Transfers tab.

11 Common questions & problems

Click a question to expand.

The Transfer button is grey / disabled. Why?

The letter must be finalized first. The order is always: Save Draft → Finalize & File PDF → Transfer staff now. Also, only the sending lobby (or admin) can press it.

It says a staff "already has a pending transfer request". What do I do?

That staff has an older transfer request that was never accepted or rejected — possibly created months ago from the biodata form. Nothing was created for anyone on your letter (all-or-nothing). Ask the lobby that request points to (or the admin) to accept/reject the old request from Dashboard → Staff Transfers, then press Transfer again.

I finalized a letter and then noticed a mistake.

If the staff have not been transferred yet: ask the Division Admin to open the letter and revert it to Draft (the filed PDF is removed automatically). Fix and finalize again. If they have been transferred: the letter is permanent — prepare a fresh corrective letter, and handle any wrongly-created requests from the Staff Transfers inbox (reject them).

The CMS ID I entered while accepting is rejected.

Two common causes: the format must be letters immediately followed by digits (KYN1234 — no spaces or hyphens), and the CMS ID must not already belong to another staff. The error message under the staff's name says which it is. A staff returning to a lobby may be given back their own old CMS ID — that is allowed.

Why is footplate KM empty on some letters?

It is optional. Lobbies that track footplate kilometres fill it; others leave it blank and the column simply prints empty — same as manual letters today.

The letter runs to two pages. Is that a problem?

No. Up to ~15 staff fit on one page. Longer letters continue on page 2 with the table header repeated and the signature at the end — standard practice. The on-screen preview shows page 1 only, with a "continues on page 2" mark at the bottom edge; the Print and PDF versions are always complete.

Who can see a letter?

Drafts: only the sending lobby (and admin). After transfer: the sending lobby (Prepared tab), the receiving lobby (Received tab), and the filed PDF in Documents is visible to all logged-in portal users — like any other document there. Other lobbies cannot open the letter page entry itself.

Does preparing a letter change anything for the staff immediately?

No. Drafting and finalizing change nothing anywhere. Even "Transfer staff now" only creates pending requests. A staff's lobby actually changes at the moment the receiving lobby accepts them with a new CMS ID — exactly like the old process.

What about Inter-Railway transfers (out of the division)?

They are deliberately not on this page — an Inter-Railway transfer has no receiving lobby in the division. Use the staff's biodata form → Transfer, as before.
