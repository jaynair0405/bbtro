📖 Understanding the Analysis
🚴 Wheel Movement:
Net movement after deducting cancellations and Sunday restrictions
⏰ Duty Hours:
Total duty hours from ML89A data - unaffected by cancellations
📊 Daily Breakdown:
Click ➕ to see day-by-day wheel movement and duty hours
🎯 Efficiency Ratio:
Compare wheel movement hours vs total duty hours
💡 Key Insights:
  • Duty Hours: Include all activities - driving, rest periods, preparation time
  • Wheel Movement: Actual train driving time (subset of duty hours)
  • Cancellations: Reduce wheel movement but NOT duty hours
  • Sunday Restrictions: Affect wheel movement calculations only
  • Efficiency: Higher wheel movement/duty hours ratio indicates better utilization
