## 1. Scope and evidence

Build MTC CLA first. MTC KYN and DTC KYN share the workflow but have their own courses and document formats; the user will supply these later.

The operational workflow is: lobby prepares letter → centre accepts → centre records attendance → centre confirms completion. Only Motorman Refresher and MEMU Refresher have advance centre batch planning visible to lobbies. Do not introduce automatic batch reassignment, compulsory advance scheduling for other courses, or revised letters generated by the centre.

### Trainee sources (confirmed 15 September 2026)

- Existing staff use `div_staff_master`.
- CLI details use `div_cli_master`; a CLI must not be excluded merely because there is no corresponding staff-master row.
- CRTMS has no Train Manager master data. The training centre enters Train Manager details manually in letters or the trainee register.
- Required manual Train Manager fields: name, CMS ID, lobby and PF number. HRMS ID is optional. Do not require division or a designation field as additional mandatory input.
- Trainee identity must support all three sources and retain its source reference. Do not invent HRMS IDs or insert placeholder staff-master records to satisfy existing foreign keys.
- Store the trainee details used for an issued letter as a snapshot so subsequent master changes do not alter historical letters.
- Proposed implementation: use a trainee identity and completion history independent of the staff-master HRMS key, with a compatibility link for actual staff-master trainees. Final keys and constraints require schema review.

### Date rules

Next due date = actual completion date + calendar validity interval − 1 day. The validity interval is calendar months/years; it is not a count of working days. Do not shift a due date because it falls on a holiday. Working-day rules apply to course scheduling and permissible joining dates.

Example: Automatic completed 15 September 2026 → next due 14 March 2027. A three-year qualification completed on that date → next due 14 September 2029.

Motorman Promotion creates two renewal effects from its MTC completion: Motorman Refresher and Automatic. Preserve the fact that the source was Promotion; do not represent this as attendance at a separate One-Day Automatic course. CLI handling is a subsequent stage and does not delay or reset those due dates. Detailed CLI-handling entry screens and ownership have not been agreed.

## 3. Planning and nomination

- Centre sets capacity for each planned batch.
- Motorman Refresher and MEMU Refresher plans are visible to lobbies.
- Suggested lobby seat allocations for longer refresher courses reflect staff strength. They are flexible targets, not fixed quotas.
- CRTMS suggests staff based on due dates; the lobby picks nominees and may also choose eligible staff outside the suggestions, including early nominations.
- Missing training history displays “Not recorded” and does not prevent nomination.
- One-Day Automatic has no advance schedule. Lobbies choose any day except Sundays and centre holidays, before, on or after the due date.
- A full batch is handled by the centre, which decides on the next batch. Do not automatically move nominees or rewrite their letters.
- Non-refresher courses must not require an advance calendar slot. Any internal grouping for attendance is an implementation detail, not an additional nomination approval stage.

## 4. Letters and acceptance

Use the current approved MTC CLA print layout for every MTC CLA course, changing course wording and document requirements. The current sample has office heading, number/date, addressee, subject, reporting instruction, staff table, signature and copy endorsement.

Recurring-course letters add Last Training Date and Due Date for every nominee, not only overdue staff. Blank history reads “Not recorded.” Use an appropriate staff/designation heading for Train Managers, LPS and CLI rather than hard-coded “Motorman Name.” Exact requirements text for newly added courses remains to be supplied or confirmed.

- Lobbies nominate directly; no division approval stage.
- Lobby edits are allowed before centre acceptance.
- Accepted letters are locked against lobby edits.
- Centre may return a letter with a reason; lobby corrects and resubmits.
- Centre may accept the entire letter or make decisions for individual nominees.
- Proposed handling of mixed decisions: preserve accepted nominee rows and letter content; allow correction/resubmission of returned nominees only. The precise user interface for mixed letters still needs review. Do not allow a returned nominee to unlock accepted trainees' data.
- Preserve submitted versions and the training dates shown when issued, so historical prints are reproducible.
- MTC KYN/DTC KYN formats remain pending; do not apply CLA wording to them by default.

## 5. Attendance, assessment and completion

Daily attendance is required for multi-day courses. One-Day Automatic requires attendance on the day. For multi-day courses the user permits one or two days' absence; longer leave/absence requires the next course and a fresh lobby letter. Record the earlier attempt and attendance without renewing its qualification.

Do not silently invent finer rules about consecutive versus cumulative absence or separate short-course thresholds. If those distinctions affect implementation, resolve them with the centre. Completion remains a manual centre decision, not an automatic attendance-percentage calculation.

- Record actual joining and actual completion dates per trainee.
- Course-level configuration supports written/oral assessments, maximum marks and passing marks.
- Actual assessment patterns and numeric thresholds remain to be entered; no defaults have been approved.
- Centre reviews attendance and required results and manually confirms successful completion.
- For failure, centre decides re-examination versus repeating the course. Re-examination links to the same attempt; repeating requires a fresh nomination letter.
- Only successful completion updates training history and due dates.
- Completed records may be corrected by the centre with mandatory reason, actor, timestamp and before/after history. Recalculate affected renewal dates without erasing later valid training.
- A printable completion statement will include trainees, marks, results, completion dates and due dates. The existing centre format will be supplied later; layout is pending.

### Integrity issues to address during implementation

1. Letter update/delete and single-letter reads need resource-level office permissions, not merely division login. Centre letter detail also needs centre scope enforcement. These are source findings, not a live exploit test.
2. Acceptance and lobby edits must be checked in one transaction so simultaneous requests cannot bypass locking.
3. Completion must verify every trainee belongs to that accepted letter/attempt before inserting records. Currently the row update result is not checked before a training insert.
4. Repeated completion requests must have one durable source key; checking staff/type/date alone does not provide complete concurrency protection or correction provenance.
5. A letter must not become wholly completed when only part of its roster has final results.
6. Accepted attendance/history must survive edits, returns and corrections. Stop delete-and-reinsert nominee updates for records with downstream activity.
7. Backdated/corrected completion must not overwrite a more recent qualifying event. Current “latest done date” selection needs review alongside reversal/supersession handling.

## 7. Proposed database approach

Names below are design proposals, not executed DDL. Prefer additive changes, stable legacy IDs and compatibility with existing biodata/history readers.

| Proposed structure | Purpose and essential relationships |
|---|---|
| Course definitions and versioned rules | Distinct Promotion/Conversion/Refresher identities; duration, eligibility, effective version and renewal effects |
| Centre course offerings | Which centre offers each course; reporting time; permitted planning and format configuration |
| Assessment definitions | Written/oral components with maximum/passing marks tied to course rule version |
| Extend training calendar | Planned refresher batch, centre-set capacity and expected end date |
| Batch lobby allocations | Suggested shares, without hard lobby quotas |
| Centre holidays | Unique centre/date with reason and audit attribution; Sundays calculated automatically |
| Letter versions and nominee decisions | Submitted snapshot, accepted/returned decision, reason, actor/time; preserve existing letter and staff IDs |
| Trainee identities | Staff-master reference, CLI-master reference or manually entered Train Manager; required manual name/CMS ID/lobby/PF number, optional HRMS ID |
| Trainee attempts | Stable link to nominee, course version, optional planned batch, actual joining/completion, outcome and prior attempt |
| Daily attendance | Unique attempt/date with status, marker and correction history |
| Assessment attempts/results | Marks and results per assessment, including re-examinations |
| Completion events and renewal effects | Source attempt, confirmed completion, training record links and each renewal target; Promotion can affect two targets |
| Audit events | Before/after values, mandatory correction reason, actor/time and affected source |

Keep `div_training_records` as the compatibility surface where practical. Its existing type IDs may represent renewal categories rather than distinct courses. Add source-course/attempt provenance and an explicit renewal mapping rather than splitting IDs blindly. The exact bridge requires current data and consumer audit.

The promotion-triggered Automatic renewal must identify Promotion as its source wherever last training/due information is shown. This avoids claiming the trainee attended a standalone Automatic course. Centre completions can update shared training data while manual biodata entry remains available; the separate profile remains a report.

## 10. Acceptance checks

- Sunday/centre holiday nomination is rejected for Automatic; another permissible day needs no planned slot. Being overdue does not prevent nomination.
- Refresher suggestions use due dates, while the lobby can pick an early nominee or staff with missing history.
- An ineligible designation warns and still permits submission for centre decision.
- Full batch handling remains a centre decision; no automatic move or centre-written replacement letter.
- Accepted nominees cannot be changed through UI or API; returned nominees can be corrected without altering accepted records.
- Daily attendance persists across reloads; long-absence repeat links to a fresh nomination. Automatic absence cannot result in successful completion.
- Trainees with different completion dates receive different due dates in the same letter.
- Promotion at MTC completion renews Motorman Refresher and Automatic; Motorman Refresher alone does not renew Automatic.
- One-time courses retain completion history with no invented renewal date.
- Leap-day/month-end dates follow one tested calendar-addition rule; holiday exclusion does not change validity intervals.
- Re-examination records retain earlier marks. Repeat courses use fresh nominations.
- Duplicate completion requests and concurrent edits do not duplicate or lose records.
- Corrections retain original data, reason and actor; a backdated correction does not displace later valid training.
- Cross-office/centre reads and writes are denied unless authorised.
- Browser verification covers lobby submission, centre acceptance/return, attendance, marks, completion, biodata refresh and printed letters. Syntax checks alone are insufficient.
